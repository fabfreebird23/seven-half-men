How it would run

In place of the flat cover on Home

The scene is the photo, the cover is the frame. The towers are the live playoff sim (the Playoffs page's numbers, cached 15 minutes); the star is whoever the week's cover picked, and the cover lines are that issue's own.

The deal drives the look. The week's skin sets the star's print, the wordmark's face and colour, the box colours and the scene's accent: the sun, the star's tower and the corners. A run of top scores still looks different every week.

Only the star gets the cover treatment. Everyone else stays a Tron tower with a face and a percentage; the star's tower is lit in the week's ink and stands in front of his print.

The archive stays a rack of flat covers. Fifteen live 3D scenes on one page would be heavy on a phone; each week's flat cover is what gets kept.

Phone: the stage turns portrait, the wordmark centres over the scene and the cover lines drop to the bottom. Drag turns the camera; double-tap replays the rise. Reduced motion shows the finished frame.
