Bloody Sunday in motion

7½ Men · ten 3D motions · five ported, five built for this league’s rules

The app has zero 3D motion today — Kreeper and the Draft Room both got theirs on 25 October and this one didn’t. The first five below are those, in the Bloody Sunday palette. The last five exist because 7½ Men has rules the other two leagues don’t: two weighted drums, a taxi clock, a franchise tag, and a pot with a cap it can overflow. Everything stands still under prefers-reduced-motion.

7½ only07The pot, overflowing

The bowls say how much. This says what happens next: fills to the cap, and everything past it visibly runs off the side into the bracket. You are sitting exactly on the cap this week, so the next dollar anyone bids starts that spill.

7½ only08Taxi carousel

Two slots on a turntable, each showing its year on the two-year clock. Year two lights amber because it is promote-or-release — a deadline that currently reads as a number in a table.

7½ only09Franchise stamp

One slot, once, and it freezes his price at the most expensive round ever paid. It should land like a decision, not like ticking a box — so it presses down the Z axis and the page shakes.

7½ only10The Chase, lit

Both brackets on one tilted plane, seats dropping in off the Z axis. The four who miss the playoffs are the ones that glow, because in this league they are playing for real money and for lottery balls — which no bracket anywhere draws.

What I would ship first. 03 split-flap and 05 towers, because Live is the page people open on a Sunday and both work with the data already on it. Then 01 and 02, which are one CSS block plus the delegated handler Kreeper already has. 06 the drums and 07 the pot are the two worth building from scratch — they are the only places this league looks like nothing else.

What is missing before 01 can ship properly. The fight-night cover wants manager photos, and 7½ Men has no assets/managers. Kreeper’s cutouts came from macOS Vision subject masks saved as transparent WebP — portable code, but it needs eight actual photos.
