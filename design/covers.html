How it runs in the app

One cover, every Tuesday

Fifteen looks, fifteen triggers. Some are rare and win the week outright when they fire: the championship issue (once a year), In memoriam (a team mathematically out), Clinched, and The field (end of the regular season). The rest compete on heat like the first six; a quiet week falls back to The issue, the table-of-contents cover.

It picks the week's biggest story from the facts Week in Review and the Game of the Week already work out: the top score, the Chase, the biggest mover, the Game of the Week, the worst bench, the luckiest win.

Then a style that hasn't run lately, so the rack never repeats two weeks running, and writes the cover lines from the numbers.

The cover goes on Home in place of the Game of the Week poster, and the same image is the group-chat recap you download from Live.

The marker line is real text: a team name, or a line the commissioner types in for the week.

Every manager has four cut-outs ready (white sticker, blue sticker, black-and-white, xerox), made by the photo script that already builds the duotones.
